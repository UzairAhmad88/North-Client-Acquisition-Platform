# Commercial Renewals & Expansion Pipeline

## 1. Closed-Loop Lifecycle Integration

Expansion opportunities discovered through Customer Success integrate into upstream commercial modules:

```text
Identified Opportunity
        ↓
Discovery Session (Phase 22)
        ↓
Client Requirements (Phase 22)
        ↓
Solution Design (Phase 23)
        ↓
Project Estimate (Phase 24)
        ↓
Proposal (Phase 23)
        ↓
Contract & Baseline (Phase 25)
        ↓
Project Execution (Phase 26)
```

## 2. Renewal Preparation

Renewal workflows track contract expiration dates, probability weighting, and contract retainers, triggering proactive CSM reviews 90 days before expiration.
