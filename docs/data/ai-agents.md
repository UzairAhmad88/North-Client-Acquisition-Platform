# Autonomous Data AI Workforce
15 specialized data agents:
1. `DiscoveryAgent`: Identifies undocumented sources and schema structures.
2. `IngestionAgent`: Optimizes and schedules data connectors.
3. `QualityAgent`: Discovers nulls, outliers, and distribution anomalies.
4. `SchemaAgent`: Manages schema drift and contract migrations.
5. `LineageAgent`: Reconstructs end-to-end data lineage.
6. `CatalogAgent`: Auto-tags and documents datasets.
7. `MetadataAgent`: Curates business metadata and glossary terms.
8. `SemanticAgent`: Maps physical columns to business entities.
9. `GraphAgent`: Traverses and enriches knowledge graph relationships.
10. `GovernanceAgent`: Audits policy compliance and access grants.
11. `PrivacyAgent`: Scans for PII and recommends masking policies.
12. `AnomalyAgent`: Detects volume anomalies and pipeline latency.
13. `OptimizationAgent`: Generates partitioning and indexing recommendations.
14. `DocumentationAgent`: Synthesizes automated summaries and READMEs.
15. `DataProductAgent`: Packages curated datasets into SLA-governed products.
