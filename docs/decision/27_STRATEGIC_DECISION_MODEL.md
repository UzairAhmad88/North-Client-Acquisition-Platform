# Strategic Decision Data Model

## Executive Summary
Structured decision formulation capturing objectives, options, assumptions, evidence, and recommendations.

## Architectural Principles
- **Decision Support, Not False Certainty**: Continuous exposure of assumptions, confidence intervals, and prediction intervals (P10/P50/P90).
- **Closed-Loop Decision Cycle**: 14-stage loop: `Question -> Data -> Context -> Forecast -> Scenarios -> Simulation -> Optimization -> Risk -> Sensitivity -> Recommendation -> Human Decision -> Execution -> Outcome -> Learning`.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Strategic Gates**: Mandatory dual-control executive sign-off required for strategic acquisitions, major capital allocations, and organizational restructurings.
- **Cross-Phase Digital Twin Bindings**: Direct synchronization with Phase 72 (Finance), Phase 73 (Trust OS), and Phase 74 (Global Operations OS).

## Functional Capabilities
1. **Digital Twin & State Engine**: Continuous multi-layer state representation distinguishing actuals from predictions.
2. **Forecast & Causal Models**: Probabilistic ensembles and driver tree elasticity calculations.
3. **Simulation & Stress Testing**: 10,000-iteration Monte Carlo runs and reverse stress breach evaluations.
4. **Pareto Optimization**: Multi-objective frontier analysis balancing profit, growth, and systemic risk.
5. **Executive War Room & Briefs**: Crisis tabletop simulation and structured one-page decision briefs.
