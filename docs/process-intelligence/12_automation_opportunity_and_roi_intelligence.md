# Automation Opportunity and ROI Intelligence

## Feasibility & Suitability Scoring
Evaluates each activity across:
- Transaction volume and standardization level
- Structured input/output predictability
- Exception frequency and cognitive complexity
- Suitable technology: RPA, API Integration, Workflow Rules, AI Agent, Human-in-the-loop

## Financial Modeling
- Upfront implementation capital expenditure (CAPEX)
- Annual recurring maintenance and AI model subscription (OPEX)
- Payback period (months) and Net Present Value (NPV)
