# Recommendation Scoring Formula & Component Dimensions

## Weighted Relevance Formula

$$\text{Relevance Score} = (\text{Business Fit} \times 0.25) + (\text{Problem/Gap Fit} \times 0.30) + (\text{Audit Evidence} \times 0.20) + (\text{Research Evidence} \times 0.10) + (\text{Category Fit} \times 0.10) + (\text{Lead Context} \times 0.05)$$

---

## Relevance Bands

| Score Range | Band Name | Operational Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **80 – 100** | `STRONG` | Exceptional service fit backed by explicit gaps or operational alignment |
| **60 – 79** | `GOOD` | Strong catalog relevance with multiple supporting signals |
| **40 – 59** | `POSSIBLE` | Plausible general fit based on business category |
| **0 – 39** | `WEAK` | Low relevance or minimal alignment |
