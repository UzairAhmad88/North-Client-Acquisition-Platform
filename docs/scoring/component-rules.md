# Component Scoring Rules

## 1. Website Need (25%)
- Evaluates requirement for website design or rebuild.
- `No Website`: 100 pts.
- `Website Gaps` (missing HTTPS, missing viewport, missing title/meta description): 30–80 pts.
- `Healthy Website`: 10–20 pts.

## 2. Online Presence (15%)
- Evaluates online visibility and research footprint.
- `No social profiles`: +50 pts.
- `Limited social presence` (1–2 profiles): +30 pts.
- `Research records present`: +10 pts.

## 3. Lead Capture Opportunity (15%)
- Evaluates missing lead conversion tools.
- `No contact form`: +50 pts.
- `No CTA buttons`: +40 pts.
- `Limited CTAs`: +20 pts.

## 4. Automation Potential (20%)
- Evaluates manual process gaps.
- `Manual contact processes`: +40 pts.
- `Performance bottlenecks`: +15 pts.
- `Target Industry` (Tech, Services, E-commerce): +15 pts.

## 5. Business Activity (10%)
- Evaluates operational signals.
- `ACTIVE CRM status`: 80 pts.
- `INACTIVE`: 40 pts.
- `Recent research activity`: +15 pts.

## 6. Contactability (10%)
- Evaluates direct public contact options.
- `Public Phone`: +40 pts.
- `Public Email`: +40 pts.
- `Location Recorded`: +20 pts.

## 7. Service Fit (5%)
- Evaluates alignment with North's Service Catalog.
- `Active Service Catalog available`: 75 pts.
- `Target Industry alignment`: +25 pts.
