# Incident Response & SOC Operations

## 1. Incident Lifecycle
$$\text{DETECT} \rightarrow \text{TRIAGE} \rightarrow \text{INVESTIGATE} \rightarrow \text{CONTAIN} \rightarrow \text{ERADICATE} \rightarrow \text{RECOVER} \rightarrow \text{VERIFY} \rightarrow \text{POSTMORTEM}$$

## 2. Severity Classification
- `CRITICAL`: Immediate threat to platform integrity or confidential data.
- `HIGH`: Active anomaly or compromised credential.
- `MEDIUM`: Policy violation or suspicious probe.
- `LOW` / `INFORMATIONAL`: Routine alert or low-confidence anomaly.
